# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js. The user prioritises the visual experience: the first two or three sections are scroll-driven and heavily animated, and the sections after them carry the information.

## Users

- Families in west Hyderabad (Kondapur, Gachibowli, Miyapur, HITEC City), often visiting Sarath City Capital Mall for shopping or a film and deciding where to eat.
- IT-corridor professionals planning team lunches, celebrations and office outings.
- Groups celebrating occasions such as birthdays and anniversaries.

The job: decide this buffet is worth it, understand the price, and book a table.

## Product Purpose

Rebranded landing page for The Grand Line, a multi-cuisine buffet. Success means visitors understand the price and the spread, and they book.

## Positioning

The current positioning is a premium multi-cuisine buffet: "A Feast of Flavors, Crafted to Perfection" and "Where every dish tells a delicious story". The rebrand target is bright, warm, premium and family-friendly. Direct competitor: Masterpiece in Gachibowli (atmasterpiece.com), which positions itself on sheer scale.

## Operating Context

- Located inside a large mall, so parking, the mall entrance, the floor and lifts are practical concerns.
- Booking currently happens by phone. No online booking system is confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pricing is two tiers, Veg and Non-veg. **The amounts are not yet provided and must not be invented.**
- A dish count exists. **The number is not yet provided.**
- Live counters or stations exist. **Their names and count are not yet provided.**
- A bar is available, and the current site has a Liquor Menu.
- Timings, a WhatsApp number and social links are not yet provided.

## Product Principles

1. The price is never hidden. A visitor knows the cost within one scroll.
2. Booking is one tap away on every screen, by phone or WhatsApp until online booking exists.
3. Food sells the experience: real photography only, no stock substitutes on the live site.
4. Feel premium without feeling expensive or formal, because families with kids must feel welcome.
