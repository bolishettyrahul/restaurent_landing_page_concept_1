# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js. The user prioritises the visual experience: the first two or three sections are scroll-driven and heavily animated, and the sections after them carry the information.

## Users

The pitch audience is a restaurant owner deciding whether to commission a new site. The site's own audience, once adopted, is:

- Families in west Hyderabad (Kondapur, Gachibowli, Miyapur, HITEC City), often visiting a mall for shopping or a film and deciding where to eat.
- IT-corridor professionals planning team lunches, celebrations and office outings.
- Groups celebrating occasions such as birthdays and anniversaries.

The job: decide this buffet is worth it, understand the price, and book a table.

## Product Purpose

A reusable concept landing page for multi-cuisine buffet restaurants, used to pitch web work to prospects. `main` carries a fictional demo brand, Harbour Buffet. Each prospect gets its own branch (for example `git checkout -b 63-degrees`) where `content/site.ts` is swapped for their real name, prices, timings, address and photos, deployed as its own site. Success for the restaurant's visitors means they understand the price and the spread, and they book.

## Positioning

Bright, warm, premium and family-friendly multi-cuisine buffet, set apart from competitors that sell on sheer scale. Per-prospect positioning and competitors are recorded on that prospect's branch.

## Operating Context

- Assumed to sit inside a large mall, so parking, the mall entrance, the floor and lifts are practical concerns.
- Booking happens by phone or WhatsApp. No online booking system; the booking panel only builds a prefilled WhatsApp message and sends nothing itself.
- Hosted on Netlify (its free plan allows commercial use; Vercel Hobby does not).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- `content/site.ts` is the only file edited per prospect. All names, contact details, prices, timings, cuisines and copy that mention the venue come from it.
- `CONCEPT_MODE` (on unless `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONCEPT_MODE=false`) makes the page noindex, blocks crawling in robots.txt, and shows the non-dismissible concept banner. Turn it off only on a site the restaurant has approved.
- Pricing is two tiers, Veg and Non-veg, plus a kids' price. Demo values: ₹799, ₹999, ₹449. A prospect's real amounts must never be invented.
- Dish count, live-counter names and timings are demo values on `main`.
- A bar section exists; drinks are priced separately.
- Theme: "The Grand Voyage", where each live counter is a port of call. No One Piece characters, logos or iconography, because that is licensed IP.
- WhatsApp messages are written out in full per occasion; never generated by trimming plurals.

## Brand Commitments

- Demo name: Harbour Buffet. Fictional; never a real brand on `main`.
- Phone, WhatsApp and email are dedicated pitch contacts, never personal ones.
- Address: a sample mall in Kondapur, Hyderabad.
- Concept credit: "Concept by Rahul, CBIT Hyderabad · Sample brand, prices and photos". On a prospect branch, add "· Not the official [Restaurant] website".
- Design references: the Dishoom (dishoom.com) hero style, and the Masterpiece (atmasterpiece.com) style of showing prices.

## Evidence on Hand

None: no photography, reviews, ratings, awards or press. Demo photos are Wikimedia Commons stock, labelled "Sample photo" and credited in the footer. Testimonials and ratings must not be fabricated, and structured data never includes `aggregateRating`.

## Product Principles

1. The price is never hidden. A visitor knows the cost within one scroll.
2. Booking is one tap away on every screen, by phone or WhatsApp until online booking exists.
3. Food sells the experience: the restaurant's own photography on any adopted site, no stock substitutes.
4. Feel premium without feeling expensive or formal, because families with kids must feel welcome.
5. A concept never passes itself off as the restaurant's official site.
